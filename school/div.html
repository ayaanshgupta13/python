The World of Sport: Cricket, Football , and Lawn Tennis Explained

Cricket

Cricket is a team sport played between two sides of eleven players each. The game revolves around a batsman hitting a ball bowled by the opposing team and running between two sets of wickets to score runs. The team with the most runs at the end wins, although they must be bowled out (lose ten wickets) first. Matches can be as brief as a few hours (Twenty20 format) or stretch for five days (Test format). The pinnacle of cricketing achievement is winning the Cricket World Cup.

Football

Football, also known as Soccer, is another team sport with eleven players on each side. Unlike Cricket, players use their feet (except for the goalkeeper) to manoeuvre a round ball and score goals by kicking it into the opposing net. The team with the most goals at the end of the match, typically lasting 90 minutes, emerges victorious. Football enjoys immense popularity across the globe, making the FIFA World Cup the most prestigious tournament in the sport.

Lawn Tennis

Lawn Tennis, on the other hand, can be played as singles or doubles. Players use a racket to hit a ball over the net and aim to land it within their opponent's court. Earning points comes by making your opponent unable to return the shot. Matches can range from quick thirty-minute affairs to gruelling encounters lasting several hours, depending on the skill level and format of play. Tennis boasts a global following, with Wimbledon Championships considered the pinnacle of the sport.
