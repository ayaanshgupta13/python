International Yoga Day

Celebrate Health and Harmony on International Yoga Day!

International Yoga Day is celebrated every year on June 21 to raise awareness about the benefits of yoga for the body and mind. The proposal for the day was introduced by India's Prime Minister, Narendra Modi, and it was officially adopted by the United Nations in 2014. Since then, people all around the globe take part in yoga sessions, discussions, and cultural events to highlight the importance of living a healthy lifestyle through yoga.

Yoga plays a crucial role in maintaining good health, reducing stress, and improving emotional balance. It combines physical postures, breathing exercises, and meditation techniques.

Benefits of Yoga

  1. Improves flexibility, strength, and posture
  2. Reduces stress and anxiety levels
    • Helps relax the nervous system
    • Promotes mental peace
  3. Enhances concentration and mindfulness

How International Yoga Day is Celebrated

  • Mass yoga events are held in parks and public spaces
  • Online yoga workshops and webinars are conducted
  • Awareness drives and campaigns promote yoga's benefits
