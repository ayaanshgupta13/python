The Taj mahal

Introduction

The Taj Mahal is one of the most beautiful monuments in the world. Located in the city of Agra, India, it was built by the Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife, Mumtaz Mahal. The Taj Mahal is widely recognized as a symbol of love and devotion, and it took around 20 years to complete, with contributions from thousands of artisans and craftsmen from across Asia.

Architecture and Design

Constructed from pristine white marble and inlaid with semi-precious stones, the Taj Mahal displays intricate calligraphy, floral patterns, and geometric designs that reflect a blend of Islamic, Persian, and Indian architectural styles. The central dome, standing at approximately 73 meters (240 feet), is one of the most impressive features, surrounded by four minarets that are slightly tilted outward to protect the main structure from earthquakes.
