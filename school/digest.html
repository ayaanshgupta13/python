Human Digestive System

Introduction

The digestive system is an essential part of the human body, responsible for breaking down the food we eat into smaller molecules that can be absorbed and used for energy, growth, and repair. This process involves both mechanical and chemical digestion, starting from the moment food enters the mouth and continuing until the waste is expelled from the body.

The system is designed to handle a variety of foods, ensuring that the body receives essential nutrients such as carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, and minerals.



Main Organs

The digestive system consists of organs such as the mouth, esophagus, stomach, small intestine, and large intestine. Each organ has a specific function that aids in digestion.
