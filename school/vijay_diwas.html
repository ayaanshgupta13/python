What is Vijay Diwas?

Vijay Diwas is observed on 16th December every year to celebrate India's victory over Pakistan in the 1971 war. This war lasted for 13 days and ended with the surrender of the Pakistani army. It is a day of great pride for every Indian, as it marks one of the most significant moments in the country's military history.Saluting the heroes of Vijay Diwas.

Why is Vijay Diwas Important?

This day reminds us of the courage and sacrifice of the Indian Armed Forces. It also led to the creation of a new country—Bangladesh. Over 90,000 Pakistani soldiers surrendered to the Indian forces, showing the strength and unity of the Indian military. Vijay Diwas teaches us to be proud of our nation's history and inspires every citizen.

Celebrations Across the Nation

On Vijay Diwas, tributes are paid at war memorials like Amar Jawan Jyoti in Delhi. Schools organize special assemblies, speeches, and cultural programs. Celebrations take place across the nation with patriotic fervor and gratitude. People remember and thank the soldiers for their sacrifices and service to the nation.
