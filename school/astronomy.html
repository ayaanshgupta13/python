International Astronomy Day

It is celebrated on 3rd May.

International Astronomy Day, celebrated on 3rd May, is a special occasion that brings together astronomy enthusiasts, educators, and curious minds from around the world. It aims to increase public awareness and interest in the wonders of the universe.

    Activities on International Astronomy Day:
  • Public telescope viewings
    • Night sky observation with expert guidance.
    • Identifying constellations and planets.
  • Short films on stars, black holes, and galaxies.
  • Hands-on experiments and model making.
    Fun Facts About Astronomy:
  1. The Sun makes up 99.8% of the mass in our solar system.
  2. Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth.
  3. Identifying constellations and planets.
